## Introduction

Wolfram Demonstrations is an interactive platform that helps students understand mathematical concepts through simulations and visualizations.

This project explores mathematical concepts related to Probability and Statistics by using interactive demonstrations. Different parameters are modified to observe their effects on probability distributions and statistical measures.

## Objectives

- To understand mathematical concepts through interactive simulations.
- To explore probability distributions and statistical measures.
- To observe changes in graphs by modifying parameters.
- To understand the behaviour of skewed distributions.
- To develop practical knowledge of mathematical concepts used in AI and Machine Learning.

## Experiments Performed

### Experiment 1: Binomial Distribution

**Module:** Probability

**Demonstration Link:**  
https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/BinomialDistribution/

**Description:**  
Studied the Binomial Distribution by changing the number of trials and probability of success.

**Parameters Used:**

| Case | Number of Trials (n) | Probability (p) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 10 | 0.5 |
| 2 | 10 | 0.2 |

**Observations:**
- When p = 0.5, the distribution is approximately symmetric.
- When p = 0.2, the distribution shifts towards fewer successes.

### Experiment 2: Mean, Median and Quartiles in Skewed Distributions

**Module:** Statistics

**Demonstration Link:**  
https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/MeanMedianAndQuartilesInSkewedDistributions/

**Description:**  
Studied the relationship between mean, median and quartiles in positively and negatively skewed distributions.

**Parameters Used:**

**Gamma Distribution:**
- a = 1
- a = 4
- a = 9
- a = 16

**Beta Distribution:**
- a = 9
- a = 4
- a = 1

**Observations:**
- In positively skewed distributions, the mean generally lies to the right of the median.
- In negatively skewed distributions, the mean generally lies to the left of the median.
- Changing the parameter affects the shape of the distribution.
